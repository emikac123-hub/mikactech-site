Company Overview

Mikac Technologies LLC is a veteran-owned software engineering firm serving government and commercial organizations. We replatform aging enterprise applications to cloud-native architectures, build event-driven data platforms, and integrate systems never designed to work together. Work is performed directly by the principal engineer, removing the coordination overhead that consumes much of a small contract's value.
